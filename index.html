Privacy Policy for Block Puzzle

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Summary: Block Puzzle is committed to protecting your privacy. We prioritize user control and transparent data handling. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect and Store

Block Puzzle is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • Local Game Progress & Preferences: Your high scores, active game grid, unlocked journey levels, star ratings, and sound/vibration toggle settings are stored locally on your device using standard Android local storage (SharedPreferences). This data stays on your device and is not sold or shared with any unauthorized third parties.
  • No Direct Personal Information: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address to play the game.

2. Third-Party Services & Integrations

To support game operations, analytics, crash diagnostics, and advertising, Block Puzzle integrates or may integrate trusted industry-standard third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third-party services collect and process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android platform integration and app updates.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising):

    Our app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process information such as your device's Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, general coarse location, device identifiers, and ad interaction telemetry to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and report ad performance.

    You can manage or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset or Delete advertising ID, or via Google Ad Settings.

    AdMob Privacy & Data Usage
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics):

    We may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Analytics (to understand game engagement, popular levels, and feature usage) and Firebase Crashlytics (to receive anonymous crash reports, stack traces, and device hardware diagnostics to fix bugs and improve game stability).

    Firebase Privacy Information

3. Device Permissions

Block Puzzle only requests permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when you place puzzle blocks and clear lines. You can disable haptics anytime in the in-game Settings.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to communicate with advertising and analytics networks (AdMob & Firebase) and to check network connectivity.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Compliance)

Our application is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child without verified parental consent, we take immediate steps to remove such information from our records.

5. User Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Union under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA), you have specific data protection rights:

  • Access & Portability: The right to request information regarding the data collected.
  • Erasure / Reset: You can reset all your locally stored game data and statistics at any time using the "Reset Progress" button in the in-game Settings screen.
  • Opt-Out of Ad Tracking: You can opt out of personalized advertising directly through your Android device settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements, game features, or third-party service integrations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
